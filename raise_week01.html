What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Think of it as a very capable colleague who has read almost everything ever written — and never gets tired, annoyed, or distracted.

It can write, analyse, summarise, brainstorm, draft, translate, explain, and more. No technical background needed to use it.

⚡
The one thing to know before you start: Claude does not remember you between conversations. Every session starts fresh. Give it context every time — and it will perform every time.

Your first 3 prompts

Do these before the Zoom call. They take 10 minutes. They will make the session 10x more useful.

01
Introduce yourself to Claude
Find out what Claude can do for your specific job
+

Copy this into Claude. Replace the bracketed parts with your real information.

I work in [your industry] as a [your job title]. My main responsibilities are [describe in 2–3 sentences]. I want to start using AI to help me at work. What are the 5 most useful things you could help me with right now?

Read the answer carefully. Does anything surprise you? Save it — we'll use it in the session.

02
Summarise something real
Use an actual document from your work this week
+

Find a long email, report, or article you need to read this week. Paste it into Claude, then write:

Summarise this in 5 bullet points. Focus on what I need to act on, not what I already know.

Notice how long that would have taken you to read. That time is yours now.

03
Ask a hard question
Bring a real problem from your work right now
+

Think of one real problem you're dealing with at work. Describe it to Claude in 2–3 sentences, then ask:

What are 3 different ways I could approach this problem? For each one, give me the main pro and the main risk.

Bring the answer to the Zoom call. We'll discuss it together.

The one rule
Garbage in.
Garbage out.

Claude is only as good as the context you give it. Vague prompt → vague answer. Specific prompt → specific, useful answer.

❌ Vague
"Write me an email about the project."
✓ Specific
"Write a follow-up email to a client who went quiet after our proposal. Warm but direct. Max 80 words."

💡 Quick tips
Start every Claude conversation by saying who you are and what you're working on.
If the answer is wrong or off — just say "that's not quite right, try again with..." Claude responds to feedback.
Paste real documents. The more context Claude has, the better it performs.
There's no wrong prompt. The only bad prompt is the one you don't send.
